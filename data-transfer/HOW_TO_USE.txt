================================================================================
DATA TRANSFER — transporte tonto (out/ + handoff/incoming/)
================================================================================

ARRANQUE
--------
  cd C:\\Alexandria\\data-transfer
  node server.js

  Variables opcionales: DT_PORT, DT_MAX_UPLOAD_MB (def 52),
  DT_MAX_SFS_MB (def 10), DT_MAX_READ_MB (def 2)

  Puerto: 4020

URL
---
  http://127.0.0.1:4020
  http://192.168.1.64:4020

RUTAS (sin interpretar cuerpo)
------------------------------
  out/              C:\Alexandria\data-transfer\out\
  incoming/         C:\Alexandria\data-transfer\handoff\incoming\

  POST /sfs                    — texto → out/{ts}.txt (límite 10 MiB)
  POST /upload                 — binario → out/ (nombre ts_original; o X-DT-Stable-Name: 1)
  POST /incoming               — binario → incoming/ (siempre ts_original)
  Cabecera: x-filename

  GET  /files?bucket=out|incoming
  GET  /read?name=&bucket=out|incoming   — vista texto cap 2 MiB
  GET  /download?name=&bucket=...
  GET  /send?text=...            — texto → out/
  GET  /latest?bucket=out|incoming
  GET  /health                   — límites y rutas (JSON)
  POST /clean-out                — borra archivos en out/
  POST /clean-incoming           — borra archivos en incoming/

  SSE GET /events
